Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 Oct 2023

1. Introduction

NEVARK LLP is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy outlines how we handle your personal data when you visit our website, use our services, or engage with us for products and consulting.

2. Information We Collect

  • Contact Details – Name, email, phone number
  • Billing Information – Payment details for purchases and services
  • Technical Data – IP address, browser, device type, and usage patterns
  • Service Inputs – Project briefs, feedback, and consultation-related materials
  • Optional Info – Survey responses, social media engagement

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Deliver and manage products, software, and services
  • Communicate updates, offers, and feedback
  • Ensure platform functionality and monitor usage
  • Conduct research and improve our offerings
  • Fulfill legal and business compliance requirements

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Trusted Vendors – For hosting, analytics, payment processing
  • Legal Authorities – If required for compliance or rights protection
  • Internal Teams – For customizing solutions or support

5. Security Measures

We maintain strict security standards—SSL encryption, access control, secure storage—to safeguard your data. However, no method is 100% secure, and we encourage users to stay vigilant.

6. Cookies & Tracking

Our website may use cookies to enhance your browsing experience. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

7. Your Privacy Rights

  • Access or correct your personal data
  • Request deletion of data we no longer need
  • Opt out of marketing communications anytime

8. Third-Party Links

We may link to external sites. We’re not responsible for their privacy practices—please review their policies independently.

9. Data Retention

We retain your data only for as long as required—by law or business needs. Once no longer needed, it’s securely deleted.

10. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect data from individuals under 18 years of age.

11. Updates to This Policy

We may revise this policy occasionally. Changes will be reflected on our website with a revised effective date.
