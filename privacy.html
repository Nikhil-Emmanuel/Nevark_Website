Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Nevark LLP (“Nevark”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) is committed to respecting your privacy and protecting any personal information shared with us. As a service-oriented company delivering custom software development, technical consulting, and hardware servicing, we handle data responsibly and only for legitimate business purposes.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to deliver our services effectively and professionally. This may include:

  • Contact Information – Name, email address, phone number, organization name
  • Business & Project Information – Project briefs, documentation, feedback, and technical inputs
  • Billing & Transaction Data – Invoices and payment references processed via secure platforms
  • Technical & Usage Data – IP address, browser type, device information, and basic analytics


3. Use of Information

The information we collect is used strictly for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Delivering and managing custom software, hardware diagnostics, and consulting services
  • Communicating project updates, timelines, and support-related information
  • Ensuring operational security, system integrity, and service quality
  • Improving internal processes, workflows, and client experience
  • Meeting legal, accounting, and compliance requirements

Nevark LLP does not sell, rent, or misuse personal information under any circumstances.

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

Nevark LLP does not sell or trade your personal information. Data may be shared only with:

  • Trusted service providers (hosting, analytics, payment processing) under confidentiality agreements
  • Internal teams directly involved in service execution or support
  • Legal or regulatory authorities when required by law


5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including secure communication channels, restricted internal access, and controlled data handling environments. While we strive to protect all data, no system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Cookies & Website Tracking

Our website may use cookies or basic analytics tools to improve performance, understand usage trends, and maintain security. Users may manage or disable cookies through their browser settings.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal and project-related data only for as long as required to complete services, fulfill legal obligations, or maintain necessary business records. Data is securely deleted or anonymized when no longer needed.

8. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or platforms. Nevark LLP is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external sites. Users should review their policies independently.

9. Children’s Privacy

Nevark LLP does not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18. Any such data identified will be promptly removed.

10. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in services, legal requirements, or operational practices. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
